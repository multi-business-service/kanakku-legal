Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026
We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). 1. DATA WE COLLECT • Account data: Name, email address, profile photo (via Google Sign-In) • Customer data: Names, phone numbers, addresses, loan details you enter • Usage data: App interactions, crash reports (anonymised) 2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA • To provide the loan tracking service • To sync your data across your devices • To send payment reminder notifications (only if enabled) • To improve app performance and fix bugs 3. DATA STORAGE Your data is stored on Supabase servers hosted in Mumbai, India. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures only you can access your data. Some of the service providers listed below (for example Google for notifications) may process limited data outside India; we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a comparable standard of protection there, consistent with the DPDP Act's cross-border transfer provisions. 4. DATA SHARING We do NOT sell your data. We do NOT share customer data with third parties for their own marketing or business purposes. We use the following processors, solely to provide the App's functionality: • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage • Firebase Analytics (anonymised) and Firebase Crashlytics — app performance and crash reports • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering payment-reminder push notifications (uses a device token, not personal content) • RevenueCat — validating subscription purchases with Google Play / Apple App Store (Pro plan only) 5. DATA YOU UPLOAD ABOUT YOUR CUSTOMERS (BORROWERS) If you use the App to record your own customers' details, you are the Data Fiduciary for that data under the DPDP Act 2023, and Kanakku / JaguvaanS Group acts only as a Data Processor, processing it solely on your instructions to provide the App. You are responsible for: • Obtaining your customers' informed consent before entering or uploading their personal data, including identity documents (e.g. Aadhaar, PAN) or photographs • Ensuring that collection and storage of such documents complies with the DPDP Act 2023 and any applicable Aadhaar-specific regulation, including UIDAI restrictions on the storage, display, or use of Aadhaar numbers by private entities • Responding to any data-access, correction, or erasure request your customer makes to you directly 6.
