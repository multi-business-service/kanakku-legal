Delete your Kanakku account

Kanakku by JaguvaanS Group · Last updated: 6 November 2026

You can delete your Kanakku account and all of its data at any time, for free. There are two ways.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Kanakku and sign in.
  2. Tap the ⚙ Settings button (top right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account (below Sign out).
  4. Read the list of what will be deleted, tick “I understand this cannot be undone”, then tap Delete forever.

Your account and data are deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

  1. Email kaasukanakku@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Kanakku account”.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Kanakku (for Google sign-in, your Google email), so we know the request is really yours.

We will confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email or Google link) and password
  • Your business details and logo
  • All borrowers you added, with their photos and documents
  • All loans, repayment schedules, payments and the activity history
  • Signed loan agreements you uploaded
  • Your plan / subscription record in Kanakku

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: copies of deleted data can remain in our database provider’s encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which they are removed automatically. They are never restored into the app.
  • Purchases: if you ever paid for Kanakku Pro, Google Play (and our subscription processor) keep their own record of the purchase, as required for tax and accounting. We cannot delete those records; see Google’s privacy policy.

Deleting only some data

You don’t have to delete the whole account. In the app you can remove a borrower (once they have no open loans). Loans you delete are archived (hidden), not erased; to erase everything permanently, delete the account as above.

If you are a borrower

Borrower details in Kanakku are entered and controlled by the lender who uses the app. Please ask that lender to remove your details. If you can’t reach them, email us at kaasukanakku@gmail.com and we will help.
